# OD3: messaging-channel pilot (one page for the owner)

**The ask:** a 4-week pilot of a one-way channel where we post Israeli results and quota news, each post linking to the
site. It's the only real "alert" we can offer without a new platform (`nba-style.md` §2).

**Recommendation: approve, and start it on the public launch day, not before.** A public channel is itself public
sharing, so it follows D1 (launch date) and D6 (no announcement: the channel is linked from the site, not promoted).

## Telegram or WhatsApp
| | Telegram channel | WhatsApp channel |
|---|---|---|
| Reach in Israel | Smaller | Much larger |
| Who sees whom | Admins can see the subscriber list | Followers' phone numbers are hidden from admins and from each other |
| Readable without the app | Yes (web preview of a public channel) | No |
| Comments | None unless we link a discussion group (we won't) | None; reactions only |

**Recommendation: a WhatsApp channel** for reach and follower privacy. Telegram works the same way if the owner prefers it.
The rules below apply to either.

## How it works
- **Who opens it:** the owner, from their account (~15 min, once). Name: "הדרך ללוס אנג׳לס 28 – עדכונים (לא רשמי)", with
  the site's icon. No Olympic or LA28 logos.
- **Who posts:** Omer as publisher, added as an admin. The owner stays the owner of the channel.
- **What goes out, and when:**
  - **Only what is already on the site.** The site is published first, then the post.
  - Results go out only after a fact-checker's sign-off (Dana or Tamar).
  - Quota and stage news needs both fact-checkers (D4).
  - No rumours, entry lists or "expected" news.
- **Format:** Hebrew, text only (no photos or video, for copyright), self-contained so it survives forwarding:
  ```
  ג׳ודו · גרנד סלאם אבו דאבי · 29.10.2026
  רז הרשקו (מעל 78 ק״ג): מקום 2
  מה זה אומר: נקודות לדירוג IJF, לא מכסה ישירה
  מקור: <official URL> · באתר: <link to #day or #a-…>
  ```
- **Frequency:**
  - at most **one evening roundup per competition day**;
  - one immediate post only for a quota earned (after both fact-checkers);
  - the Sunday digest;
  - nothing between 23:00 and 07:00.
  That's roughly 3–5 posts a week in November.
- **Corrections:** fix the post, add a short "תיקון:" post, and log it in the site's corrections log.

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Privacy:** subscriber data, admin phone numbers | One-way channel; we collect nothing; admin numbers hidden via privacy settings. Telegram admins can see subscribers, so we never export or use them |
| **Athletes' personal information** | Only what's already on the site. No family, injury or health details |
| **Moderation** | Nothing to moderate: no comments, and no discussion group |
| **A wrong result spreads by forwarding** | Fact-checker sign-off first; every post carries source and date; a visible "תיקון:" post |
| **Impersonation or fake channels** | The channel is linked only from `#about` and the footer, and the site names it as the official unofficial channel |
| **Brand** | "לא רשמי" in the name and in each roundup; no logos |

## Effort per week
- Omer (publisher): ~10 min per competition day plus ~5 min for the digest, so **~30–45 min/week**.
- Fact-checkers: **no extra work**, since posts reuse the site sign-off.
- Dev: one-time, **S**: a channel link in `#about` and the footer (Shira or Omer).
- Owner: 15 min, once, to open the channel and add Omer.

## With the site
The site stays the source of truth, and every post links back to `#day-<date>` or the athlete page. The channel's
follower count is our **first real reach number** (metric M8). It goes in the weekly owner update.

**After 4 weeks**, the PM recommends one of: keep it, make it daily, or stop. The basis is the follower trend, the
corrections caused, and Omer's actual time spent.

## Decision
OD3: approve the 4-week pilot, starting on launch day, as a WhatsApp channel (or Telegram). **Recommended: yes.**
If there's no decision, updates stay in-site only.
