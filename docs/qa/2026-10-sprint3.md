### Issues
**R3-1 (P2). `#admin` loses focus on its `<h1>` after the runs snapshot arrives.** Editor only, 400 and 1280.
Steps: navigate to `#admin` with the keyboard.
Actual: `navigate()` focuses the `<h1>`. `subscribeRuns()` then gets its first snapshot and calls `refresh()`, which re-renders the view and leaves focus on `<body>`.
Fix: in `refresh()`, restore focus to the view's `<h1>` when it had focus, as it already does for `#q`.

**R3-2 (data gap, not a bug). The strip's stakes tie-break by world rank is inactive.** No athlete in the fixture has `rankNum`/`rankAsOf` (#82 fields), so the order falls back to file order. On 29.10 the judo row shows the 60 kg and 73 kg lines first, while Raz Hershko (world #1) is under "ועוד 5". It will start working once the job fills `rankNum`. Re-test after the first export that includes it.

## 2. Test plan: `#day-<date>` and `#following` (phase 1, acceptance items 1–13)
Sources: the phase-1 spec in #130 (`docs/design/daily-and-following.md` @ 0ed0cd7) and the PM's acceptance list in #131 (`docs/product/nba-style.md`). Case numbers are the PM's items 1–13.
**Copy:** assertions use the final strings from the spec's §4 (Shira). They replace the acceptance list's wording: "לפני / מתחרים עכשיו / ממתינים לתוצאה" becomes "לפני התחרות / מתחרה היום / ◷ בהמתנה לתוצאה". Status is computed with `SESSION_WINDOW_H = 3`, from `startUtc`.

### Preconditions and fixtures
The current fixture has no `sessions`, no `result` history entries and no `rankNum`. Without these fixtures, items 2–5 and 10 can't be tested at all.
- **F1 `sessions`:** a fixture extending `tests/fixtures/data.json`, owned by Maya (1.2) and reviewed by QA. It needs:
  - 29.10 (Abu Dhabi): 3 judo sessions on different days, one with `startUtc` at 09:30 Israel time, one without, and one with a non-empty `result` and `resultAt`.
  - 3.11 (Doha): one shooting session with a time.
  - 30.10: a day with sessions in a sport the viewer follows and one they don't.
  - An empty day: 25.10 after Rotterdam is filtered, or a day with no sessions at all.
  - A `result` field containing `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>`, plus a `resultSource` that uses `http://`.
- **F2 `result` history:** one athlete with `kind:"result"` dated 29.10, and one with `updatedAt` newer than `la28.seen`.
- **F3 follows:** set through the UI only (☆), never by writing to `localStorage` directly. A blocked-storage run uses the init script that throws on `localStorage`.
- **Clock:** fake dates 29.10 with a session at 09:30 Israel time, pinned at 08:00 (before), 10:00 (inside the 3 h window) and 13:00 (after); 3.11; and the empty day. Also run once with `sessions` absent, to cover the spec's fallback (pseudo-sessions from `nextDate` and `result` history; times say "תאריך בלבד").
- **Matrix:** signed out, 400px and 1280px, light and dark. Editor mode only to confirm there's no difference beyond the editor chip.
