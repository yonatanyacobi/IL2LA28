## Qualification system (ITF, LA28)
- **Field:** 86 per gender, including 3 host and 1 universality place. Singles draws have 64. Max 6 athletes per gender per NOC and max 4 per singles event.
- **Ranking date:** the official ATP and WTA singles and doubles rankings of **2028-06-12**, the first ranking after Roland Garros. NOCs confirm by 2028-06-20.
- **Singles (64 per gender)** = 56 direct acceptances by ranking + 1 host place + 6 ITF places + 1 universality place.
  - The 6 ITF places are **4 continental + 2 champions**:
    - 4 continental: the singles winners of the 2026 Asian Games and the 2027 Pan American, African and Pacific Games. None is for Europe, so Israel has no continental route.
    - 2 for players without direct acceptance who have won Olympic singles gold or a Grand Slam singles title.
  - Both kinds of ITF place require a ranking inside the top 500.
- **Doubles:** 32 teams = 31 direct + 1 host, max 2 per NOC. Players in the doubles top 10 get priority, then teams by Combined Ranking (each player's best singles or doubles ranking). Mixed doubles: 16 teams = 15 direct + 1 host, and both players must already be in singles or doubles.
- **Eligibility (unverified):** a player must be on the nominated and present Davis Cup or BJK Cup team at least twice in the Olympic cycle, at least once in 2027 or 2028.
  This comes from the search snippet of the ITF eligibility rule; the PDF now returns 404.
- Sources:
  - ITF qualification system (version 12 May 2026; read in full): https://www.itftennis.com/media/15928/tennis-la28-qualification-system.pdf
  - Eligibility rule, not readable (404): https://www.itftennis.com/media/14173/la-2028-olympic-tennis-event-eligibility-rule.pdf
