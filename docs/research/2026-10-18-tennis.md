## Qualification system (ITF, LA28)
- **Field:** 86 per gender, including 3 host and 1 universality place. Singles draws have 64. Max 6 athletes per gender per NOC and max 4 per singles event.
- **Ranking date:** the official ATP and WTA singles and doubles rankings of **2028-06-12**, the first ranking after Roland Garros. NOCs confirm by 2028-06-20.
- **Singles:** 56 direct acceptances by ranking, then 6 ITF places:
  - 4 continental places (Asian, Pan American, African and Pacific Games). None of these is for Europe, so there is no continental route for Israel.
  - 2 places for former Olympic or Grand Slam singles champions.
  - 1 host place and 1 universality place.
  - All ITF places require a ranking inside the top 500.
- **Doubles:** 32 teams, max 2 per NOC. Players in the doubles top 10 get priority, then teams by Combined Ranking (each player's best of singles or doubles ranking). Mixed doubles has 16 teams, and both players must already be in singles or doubles.
- **Eligibility:** a player must be on the nominated and present Davis Cup or BJK Cup team at least twice in the Olympic cycle, with at least one of those in 2027 or 2028.
- Sources:
  - https://thebridge.in/tennis/tennis-olympic-qualification-system-la28-itf-55919 (summary of the ITF document)
  - ITF system PDF: https://www.itftennis.com/media/15928/tennis-la28-qualification-system.pdf
  - Eligibility rule: https://www.itftennis.com/media/14173/la-2028-olympic-tennis-event-eligibility-rule.pdf
  - The ITF PDFs are behind a bot-check, so their content was taken from the search snippet and the summary above.
