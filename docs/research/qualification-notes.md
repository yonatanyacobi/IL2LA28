# How to read a qualification system: basketball, 3x3, shooting, golf, fencing

For UX (later About content). Written 18.10.2026 from the official LA28 qualification systems (the IOC documents; copies are linked where olympics.com isn't reachable).
"Max for Israel" is the system's per-country limit. "Where Israel stands" only states facts we already track, with no forecasts.

## Basketball (5x5)
- **Places:** 12 teams per gender, 1 per country.
- **Men:**
  - **World Cup 2027, Qatar (27.8–12.9.2027):** 7 teams qualify directly, 2 of them European. The best of the rest go to the Olympic Qualifying Tournaments (OQT).
  - **Olympic pre-qualifiers (August 2027):** for teams like Israel that played the second round of the World Cup qualifiers but miss the World Cup. Europe sends 2 to the OQT.
  - **Four OQTs (26.6–2.7.2028):** each winner qualifies.
- **Women:**
  - The 2026 World Cup champion qualifies directly.
  - Everyone else goes through the OQTs in February 2028 (10 places).
  - Europe sends the **top 6 of EuroBasket Women 2027** (16–27.6.2027), whose qualifiers run in Nov 2026 and Feb 2027.
- **Rankings that count:** none directly; it's all tournaments. The FIBA ranking only fills replacements.
- **Max for Israel:** 1 team per gender.
- **Where Israel stands:**
  - Men: 6th (last) in group K of the World Cup qualifiers, 2–6.
  - Women: in group I of the EuroBasket 2027 qualifiers. The top two go to EuroBasket, where a top-6 finish is needed to reach the OQT.
- **Documents:**
  - Men: https://cdn.nocnsf.nl/media/kcgkdtvi/bkb-la28-qualification-system.pdf
  - Women: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/our-sport/basketball/national-team-competition-systems/olympic-womens-basketball-tournaments

## 3x3 basketball
- **Places:** 12 teams per gender (4 players each), 1 per country.
- **Routes:**
  1. **NOC ranking of 1.12.2027:** 5 places, at most 1 for Europe. It sums the top 20 national players' FIBA 3x3 points over two years (to 1.12.2026 and to 1.12.2027).
  2. **Universality OQT (2028):** 2 places, open only to countries that didn't play 5x5 basketball at the last two Olympics. That includes Israel.
  3. **Ranking OQT (2028):** 2 places.
  4. **Olympic Q-Series:** 2 places (Tokyo 4–7.5.2028, Shanghai 11–14.5.2028).
- **Max for Israel:** 1 team per gender.
- **Where Israel stands:** no national-team ranking tracked. The realistic path is the universality OQT, which also depends on the NOC ranking for the invitation.
- **Document:** https://cdn.dosb.de/Relaunch_2024/Athletenservice/Games/LA_2028/Internationale_Qualikriterien/Updates_21072026/BK3-LA28-Qualification-System_20260710.pdf

## Shooting
- **Places:** 340 (170 per gender) across 12 individual events and 3 mixed teams. Mixed teams use athletes already qualified individually.
- **How a place is won:** by finishing high enough at a designated competition, not by world ranking.
  - Places go in final order (then qualification order), skipping countries already holding one there.
  - The athlete must also have shot the minimum score (MQS; 613.0 in 10m air rifle).
- **Key dates:**
  - World Championship Doha: 1–14.11.2026. Air rifle on 3.11, with 3 places per event and 1 per country.
  - World Championships 2027: Cairo shotgun 23.9–2.10.2027; Daegu rifle/pistol 28.10–9.11.2027.
  - World Cups through 2027.
  - European Championships 2027 and 2028, and the European Games 2027 (Istanbul): Europe's continental places, 5 per event.
  - **Olympic Ranking of 1.5.2028:** one last place per event.
- **Max for Israel:** 2 per individual event (1 per event at a World Championship); 12 per gender.
- **Where Israel stands:** Richter (men's air rifle, 55 in the ISSF World Ranking) and Tashtchiev (women's air rifle, 22) are entered for Doha.
- **Document:** https://www.asia-shooting.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Qualification-System-01Jul26-ISSF.pdf (ISSF system, version 19.6.2026)

## Golf
- **Places:** 60 per gender, chosen by the **Olympic Golf Ranking (OGR)**.
  - The top 15 qualify, with up to 4 per country.
  - Beyond that, up to 2 per country.
  - Every continent is guaranteed a place.
- **Ranking period:**
  - Women: 8.6.2026–5.6.2028.
  - Men: 22.6.2026–19.6.2028.
  - Points come from tournaments on tours the world rankings recognise, such as the LPGA.
- **Mixed team:** countries are ranked by their best-ranked athlete, of either gender (the Olympic Mixed-Team Ranking). Both team members must already be in the individual events.
- **Max for Israel:** 2 per gender, or more only with players in the top 15.
- **Where Israel stands:** Laetitia Beck plays the LPGA in 2026 and is entered in The ANNIKA (12–15.11.2026). Her OGR position isn't verified.
- **Document:** https://www.igfgolf.org/news/2026/06/09/olympic-golf-qualification-process-officially-underway-for-la28-olympic-games

## Fencing
- **Places:** 212 (106 per gender). Per weapon: 8 teams of 3, plus 10 individual places for countries without a team.
- **Ranking period:** 1.4.2027–1.4.2028. Nothing before April 2027 counts.
- **Teams:** the FIE team ranking of 1.4.2028. The top 4 qualify, plus the best team from each of the 4 zones among places 5–24.
  A team's three fencers also enter the individual event.
- **Individual, for countries without a team:**
  - 2 places from the adjusted individual ranking.
  - 4 by zone, 1 of them for Europe.
  - 4 from zonal qualifying tournaments (2–30.4.2028), 1 of them for Europe.
- **Max for Israel:** 3 per weapon (one team), or 1 per weapon without a team.
- **Where Israel stands:**
  - The men's épée team finished 4th at the 2026 World Championships.
  - Yuval Freilich is 262 on the FIE ranking.
  - Both only start to count from 1.4.2027.
- **Document:** https://cdn.nocnsf.nl/media/3xyjpnpr/fen-la28-qualification-system.pdf

## Reading tips (for any sport)
- **The document:** find the IOC "Qualification System" document for the sport. Section B gives the places and per-country limits; section D gives the routes, in priority order.
- **Ranking vs. event:** check whether places come from a **ranking on a date** (golf, fencing, judo) or from **finishing at named events** (shooting, basketball). That decides which results matter.
- **Country vs. name:** check whether places go to the **country** or **by name**. A country place lets the federation choose the athlete.
- **Continental places:** these are the usual route for countries outside the top of a ranking. Look for "zone" or "continental" in section D.
